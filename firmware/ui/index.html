🎷JazzTrix Setup

A dashboard with jazz radio, live weather, a brew counter, and a daily haiku. A fully customizable experience, made your own.

Pair this phone

Double-tap the round button. The paper shows six digits for about five minutes. Join JazzTrix-Setup and type the code here.

Type the six digits on the display.

Wi‑Fi

The home network. After you save, the password stays on the device. This page does not keep it.

The home network. The password stays on the device.

Weather

Live weather for the place you pick. Hours ahead is the forecast: 3, 6, 9, or 12 hours. It draws as a graph, with the hours along the bottom and the temperatures on the left. Bars are the other choice. Wind can be m/s, km/h, or mph.

Search for the place that should sit on the paper.

On the paper

The large temperature and the forecast. Turn either one off. The rest of the paper stays.

Turn the temperature or the forecast off.

Borders

A frame around one part of the paper. Tiles, brew, radio, and the haiku start framed. The forecast, the degrees, and the sky line start open.

Haiku

A new poem each local day, or pin one of the 30. The lines stay in English so they fit the box.

A new one each local day, or pick one. The poems stay in English.

Brew

The words on the brew box. It can name any drink. Tap the tag on the desk to count one. The number goes up and the paper refreshes.

The words on the Brew tile. Could be any drink.

Radio

The stations the round button plays. One tap turns the radio on or off. A short press of Up or Down changes the volume. Hold one for about two seconds to change station.

The stations the round button plays.
